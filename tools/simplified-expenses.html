Simplified expenses calculator

Sole traders can claim some costs at HMRC flat rates instead of working out the real cost. This calculator works out the two most common: business mileage and working from home. It's filled in with an example, so type your own figures.

Business mileage

Mileage you can claim£4,620

Cars and vans: the first 10,000 miles at 55p (45p before 6 April 2026), then 25p a mile. Motorcycles: 24p a mile. You can't use flat rates for a vehicle you've claimed capital allowances on, and once you use them for a vehicle you must keep using them for it. Parking and train fares can be claimed on top.

Working from home

Use of home you can claim£248

That's more than 12 months. Check your figures.

Months under 25 hours can't use the flat rate. Phone and broadband aren't included, so claim their business share separately. If your real costs are higher, you can claim those instead.

Source: gov.uk, "Simplified expenses if you're self-employed", checked October 2026.
