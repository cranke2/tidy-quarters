How HMRC works it out

Filing late

  • £100 as soon as you're late, even if you owe no tax.
  • After 3 months, £10 a day for up to 90 days (£900 at most).
  • After 6 months, another 5% of the tax due or £300, whichever is more. The same again after 12 months.

Paying late

  • 5% of the tax still unpaid at 30 days, again at 6 months, and again at 12 months.
  • Interest on top from the day after the deadline. HMRC's late payment rate has been 7.75% a year since 9 January 2026. It changes with the Bank of England base rate.

If you had a reasonable excuse, such as a serious illness or a bereavement, you can appeal a penalty. If you can't pay in full, setting up a payment plan with HMRC before the deadline usually stops late payment penalties, though interest still runs.

Using Making Tax Digital from April 2026? These are the rules for 2025-26 returns. From 2026-27, people in MTD move to points and new late payment penalties, and from April 2027 the new penalties apply to everyone who files a personal Self Assessment return.
