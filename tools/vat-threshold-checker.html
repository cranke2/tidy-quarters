How the test works

  • Looking back: at the end of each month, add up your taxable turnover for the last 12 months. If it's over £90,000, register within 30 days of the end of that month. Your registration starts on the first day of the second month after you went over.
  • Looking forward: if you expect your taxable turnover in the next 30 days alone to go over £90,000, register by the end of those 30 days. Your registration starts from the day you realised.
  • Turnover, not profit. It's your sales before costs. Sales that are exempt from VAT or outside its scope don't count.
  • Mostly zero-rated? You may be able to apply for an exemption from registering.
  • Once registered, you can cancel if your taxable turnover drops below £88,000.
  • Want to know what registering would do to your prices and profit? HMRC has a free VAT Registration Estimator on gov.uk.

Sources: gov.uk, "Register for VAT: when to register" and "How VAT works: VAT thresholds", checked 24 October 2026.
