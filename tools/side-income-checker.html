Do I need to tell HMRC about my side income?

Since 2024, sites like eBay, Vinted, Etsy and Airbnb send HMRC details of anyone who sells 30 or more items, or about £1,700, in a year. That doesn't mean you owe tax. This checker shows whether you need to register, using HMRC's £1,000 allowances. It's filled in with an example.

The rules in brief

  • Selling your own unwanted things isn't trading, so there's usually no tax. Very valuable single items (over £6,000) can bring capital gains tax.
  • Trading allowance: the first £1,000 of trading income each tax year is tax-free. Go over £1,000 before expenses and you must register for Self Assessment by 5 October after the tax year ends.
  • Allowance or expenses? Once you're over £1,000, you can deduct either the £1,000 allowance or your actual costs, whichever is bigger, but not both.
  • Property allowance: another £1,000 for things like renting a driveway. Between £1,000 and £2,500 you contact HMRC; over £2,500 you register for Self Assessment.
  • Renting a room in your own home is covered separately by Rent a Room relief, which gives up to £7,500 a year tax-free.
  • You can't use the allowances for income from your employer, a partnership, or a company you or a connected person control.

Sources: gov.uk "Tax-free allowances on property and trading income" and "Information for online sellers", checked October 2026.
