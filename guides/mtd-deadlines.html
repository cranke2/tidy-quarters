MTD quarterly update deadlines

Updated October 2026 · 3 minute read

Under Making Tax Digital for Income Tax you send HMRC four quarterly updates a year, then a final declaration. The update deadlines are the same every year: 7 August, 7 November, 7 February and 7 May.

Standard quarters

QuarterCoversDue by
16 April to 5 July7 August
26 April to 5 October7 November
36 April to 5 January7 February
46 April to 5 April7 May

Each update is cumulative: it covers everything from the start of the tax year, not just the last three months. If you spot a mistake in an earlier quarter, you fix it in the next update rather than resending the old one.

Calendar quarters

You can choose quarters that end on 30 June, 30 September, 31 December and 31 March instead, which suits people whose bank statements run by calendar month. The deadlines stay the same.

The final declaration

After the year ends you confirm your figures, add any other income and make your claims. This replaces the old Self Assessment return and is due by 31 January after the tax year. Tax is still paid on the usual dates: 31 January and 31 July.
