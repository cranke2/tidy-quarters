Making Tax Digital for landlords

Updated October 2026 · 5 minute read

If your rental income, plus any self-employment income, is more than £30,000 before expenses in the 2025-26 tax year, you'll need to use Making Tax Digital from 6 April 2027. The threshold falls to £20,000 from April 2028.

Jointly owned property

Count only your share of the rent toward the threshold. A couple who own a flat 50/50 with £36,000 of rent each have £18,000, so neither is required to join in April 2027 on that alone.

Mortgage interest is different

For residential lets you can't deduct mortgage interest like other costs. HMRC gives a tax credit at 20% instead. Keep it as a separate line ("residential property finance costs") and record only the interest, never capital repayments.

Repairs vs improvements

Replacing like-for-like is a repair you can claim. Adding something new, such as an extension or a loft conversion, is an improvement and isn't a revenue expense.

Do I need expensive software?

No. A spreadsheet plus HMRC-recognised bridging software is allowed. Many small landlords with one or two properties find that simpler and cheaper than a monthly subscription.
