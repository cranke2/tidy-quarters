MTD penalties explained

Updated October 2026 · 4 minute read

Making Tax Digital for Income Tax uses a points system for late submissions and a separate system for late payment. There's no fine for a single slip, but missing several deadlines adds up.

Late updates: penalty points

Each missed deadline, whether a quarterly update or the final return, earns one point. At 4 points you get a £200 penalty, and another £200 for every deadline you miss after that. You only get one point per deadline, even if you have several businesses.

First-year break: there are no penalties for missing a quarterly update deadline in the 2026-27 tax year. You still have to send the updates before your return.

Getting rid of points

Below 4 points, each point drops off 24 months after the missed deadline. Once you reach 4, they only clear after 12 months of sending everything on time, with nothing outstanding from the previous 24 months.

In your first year under the new penalties you get 30 days instead of 15 to pay or agree a payment plan before a penalty starts. Interest is charged from day one either way.

The easiest way to avoid points

Spend ten minutes a week adding receipts and income to your records. Then each quarterly update is just a total you send through your software, not a scramble. Keep the four dates somewhere you'll see them: 7 August, 7 November, 7 February and 7 May.
