Bridging software for MTD: the spreadsheet route

Updated October 2026 · 4 minute read

Making Tax Digital doesn't force you onto a monthly accounting app. If you keep your records in a spreadsheet, a small program called bridging software reads your totals and sends them to HMRC. HMRC keeps the official list on gov.uk: search for "find software that works with Making Tax Digital for Income Tax".

How it works

You keep your income and expenses in a spreadsheet, using HMRC's categories. Each quarter the spreadsheet adds up your totals since 6 April. The bridging software opens the file, picks up those totals, and submits your quarterly update. At the end of the year you use it (or another product) for the final declaration.

What to check before you pick one

Does it handle both self-employment and property income if you have both? Does it do the final declaration, or only quarterly updates? Can it read your spreadsheet layout, or does it need a template? Is there a free trial so you can test it with your own file?
