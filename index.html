Questions people ask

Is it based on profit or turnover?

Turnover. HMRC uses your gross income from self-employment and property added together, before any expenses. Someone with £35,000 of sales and £20,000 of costs is still over the £30,000 line.

Am I allowed to use a spreadsheet?

Yes, as long as the figures reach HMRC digitally. You use HMRC-recognised bridging software, which reads the totals from your spreadsheet and sends them. You can't retype numbers by hand into another program.

What if I'm under £30,000?

Nothing changes for now. The threshold drops to £20,000 from April 2028, based on your 2026-27 income. Nothing is currently planned below £20,000.

What happens if I send an update late?

Each late quarterly update earns a penalty point. At four points you pay a £200 penalty. The first year (2026-27) had no points for late updates, but from April 2027 they count.

Does the kit send anything to HMRC?

No. The spreadsheet keeps your records and works out the totals. Your bridging software sends them. That keeps you in control and means there's no account to set up with us.

Can I be exempt?

Possibly. If you can't use digital tools because of age, disability, where you live or religious reasons, you can apply to HMRC for an exemption. Search gov.uk for "apply for an exemption from Making Tax Digital for Income Tax".
